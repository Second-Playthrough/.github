# Second Playthrough

> The first playthrough is for experiencing the game. The second is for understanding it.

**Second Playthrough** is an English-language YouTube channel of long-form video essays about **games as a medium, an experience, a cultural form and an industry**. It isn't a review or news channel: individual games appear as case studies for bigger questions. What can games do that books and films can't? What do our choices in them say about us? How does the industry shape what gets made?

This organisation holds everything behind the videos: scripts, research, voice, footage plans, edits, and the code that turns them into finished episodes, Shorts, subtitles and thumbnails.

## How the videos are made

Every episode is a copy of the [template](https://github.com/Second-Playthrough/template) and follows the same 15-step workflow, from ideation and research to script, voice, direction, footage, edit, render, subtitles and Shorts.

- **Researched.** Every claim and number is traced to a source in the episode's research ledger.
- **Narrated** with a local voice model ([Chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox)) cloning the channel's voice.
- **Made in code, on the GPU.** Animated charts and cards, synthesized music and sound effects, camera moves and effects are all generated by scripts and rendered with PyTorch on CUDA and NVENC. A change to the script or the edit means re-running a tool, not rebuilding by hand.
- **Edited from a plain-text edit list.** Each shot is anchored to the narration, uses footage that fits the script, and holds long enough to be seen.
- **Accessible.** Subtitles in English, Persian, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish.

## Working here

Humans and AI agents both work in these repos. Read the template's [`Workflow/RULES.md`](https://github.com/Second-Playthrough/template/blob/main/Workflow/RULES.md) and [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/Second-Playthrough/template/blob/main/AGENTS.md) before contributing. Anyone may suggest improvements to the template; changes are made with the channel owner's approval, so every new episode starts from the best version of the workflow.
