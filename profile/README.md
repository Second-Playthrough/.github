# Second Playthrough

> The first playthrough is for experiencing the game. The second is for understanding it.

**Second Playthrough** is an English-language YouTube channel of long-form video essays about games: as a medium, an experience, a cultural form and an industry.

It isn't a review or news channel. Individual games appear as case studies for bigger questions. What can games do that books and films can't? What do our choices in them say about us? How does the industry shape what gets made, and how do games reach beyond entertainment?

Every video aims to leave you with at least one new way of thinking about games and the entertainment world around them: familiar games, seen from a new angle.
